### 1 — Saludsa: Enterprise Email Marketing Automation
- Meta: Health Insurance · Latin America
- Stack line: OpenClaw multi-agent system · HubSpot CRM · Custom campaign pipeline
- Metric: Enterprise / Multi-agent deployment
- Challenge: Saludsa's marketing team was manually managing segmented email campaigns across thousands of policyholders and prospects. Campaign creation, A/B testing, send-time optimisation, and performance analysis were all done by hand, creating bottlenecks that slowed their go-to-market and led to inconsistent messaging across customer segments.
- Solution: Deployed an enterprise OpenClaw agent system integrated with HubSpot CRM. A Campaign Strategist agent segments the audience by profile, policy type, and engagement history and generates personalised campaign briefs. A Copywriter agent produces email sequences in Saludsa's brand voice, localised for the market. A Performance Analyst agent monitors open rates, CTR, and conversions, then feeds learnings back into the next campaign cycle, allowing the system to improve with every send. Full audit trail and human approval at each campaign launch.

### 2 — Enterprise AI Sales Agent: Agentic Orchestration & Security Stack
- Meta: Regulated Services · 30 Advisors
- Stack line: OpenClaw · n8n · LangGraph · Claude Enterprise · 5-layer architecture · 7-layer security
- Metric: 12-week / Implementation window
- Challenge: A commercial sales force of 30 advisors in a regulated industry faced three structural bottlenecks: low conversion from digital leads due to slow manual response, generic quotations that failed to resonate with individual prospect profiles, and limited follow-up productivity from manual CRM work. Sensitive end-user data had to remain entirely within the client perimeter at all times.
- Solution: Built a three-function enterprise AI Sales Agent on a five-layer architecture. Function 1 (Lead Intelligence) scrapes and scores prospects, generating a tailored sales argument per lead before the advisor engages. Function 2 (24/7 Conversion Agent) responds to inbound WhatsApp and web form leads instantly, qualifies them, and books advisor calls automatically via HubSpot. Function 3 (Offer Hyper-Personalisation) generates a fully personalised quotation and unique landing page per prospect using enriched profile data. The security stack includes AWS WAF, container isolation per agent, automatic PII tokenisation before any data reaches an orchestrator, immutable audit logging, and zero-retention Claude Enterprise contracts, meeting ISO 27001 and local data protection regulation.
- Tags: OpenClaw, n8n, LangGraph, Claude Enterprise, HubSpot, WhatsApp API, AWS WAF, PII Tokenisation, ISO 27001
