03 / KNOW WHAT TO EXPECT

Before you send

The inbox is checked every minute. Replies may be delayed by provider failures. Check Spam if a reply is missing. No reply-time guarantee is made.

Supported files and request limits

Plain email text, UTF-8 TXT/CSV, text-based PDF and XLSX with explicit values. Maximum: 3 attachments, 10 MB combined, 20 pages per PDF, 25 items and 100,000 extracted characters.

Scanned PDFs, encrypted or corrupt files and spreadsheet formulas are not supported.

Daily limits and messages that receive no reply

3 accepted requests per sender per UTC day; 50 across the demo. Requests above these limits receive no automatic reply.

Self-mail, automated messages, mailing lists, bounces and unauthenticated senders receive no reply. Use your normal email provider and send from your own account.

Demo privacy and quotation boundaries

Use synthetic information only. Do not send customer records, confidential files or sensitive personal information. Gmail, the hosted worker, OpenRouter, Anthropic Claude and Supabase process your email and supported attachments to prepare the reply.

Demo database content expires after 7 days; minimal hashed sender and deduplication metadata after 30 days. Gmail and provider retention are separate. The demo does not delete your original email.

No stock, delivery or purchase commitment is made. Production quotations require human review. Read demo privacy details.

Questions before we begin.

What should we automate first?

A repeatable task with clear inputs and a useful output. Discovery checks whether automation fits and where a person should remain involved.

Can you work with our existing tools?

We assess available APIs, access permissions and data formats during discovery. We confirm compatibility before including an integration in scope.

Can people approve actions?

Yes. Approval gates and escalation paths can be part of the agreed workflow, especially where actions have financial or customer consequences.

What happens after delivery?

Monitoring, maintenance and improvement are agreed separately. Responsibilities, support coverage and usage costs are documented before release.

How are projects priced?

Pricing follows discovery and depends on scope, integrations, data readiness and support needs. Tool and model usage costs are discussed separately.

Can agents run outside office hours?

Workflows can be configured to run on agreed triggers and schedules. Availability depends on the connected services and agreed support coverage.
